# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Owners of small businesses (practices, studios, shops, consultancies) who need a new website, a relaunch of an outdated one, or someone to keep a WordPress site running and fix it when it breaks. Roughly half are outside Austria (DACH and international), half in Austria. Edris is based in Dornbirn, but the site never targets Vorarlberg as a market (confirmed 2026-11-01).
- Agencies that need a developer for builds or for the monthly maintenance of their clients' sites.
- Recruiters and employers who use the site as a CV for a web developer.

## Product Purpose
Portfolio and lead source of Edris Husein, a freelance web developer in Dornbirn, Austria. It shows real work (case studies, articles, testimonials) and turns a visitor into an enquiry through /contact. Success: a qualified enquiry, or a hiring contact, from someone who understood what he does and trusts the proof.

## Positioning
One developer who writes the code himself and talks to the client directly, without a project manager in between. The client owns the result: domain, hosting and admin account in their name, and they can edit their own content. Fixed price for a defined scope, progress shown on a test site before launch. Generic web and solutions developer: WordPress for most business sites, Next.js or Laravel for web apps. Most of his day-to-day work is fixing and maintaining sites someone else built.

## Operating Context
- Headless WordPress (cms.edrishusein.com, ACF Pro) feeds a Next.js 16 site; every visible text is an ACF field with a code fallback.
- Every page exists in English and German (`/de`), German slugs differ for services.
- Visitors arrive from search and from shared links; contact must be one tap away (email or WhatsApp with a prefilled message, 2026-11-01).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services: website development, website redesign/relaunch, WordPress maintenance.
- No page-builder framing (never mention Elementor). The Austrian and Swiss agencies stay unnamed; exception: the employer testimonial names its agency.
- No prices, no hourly rate on the site.
- Never "Upwork". No ratings or star scores on testimonials, no dates.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Edris Husein, monogram "eh" logo, real portrait photo (`public/images/Edris-Husein-Hero.png`).
- Voice: plain, direct, first person, concrete; German uses "Sie". No hype, no sentence written for effect, no em dashes.

## Evidence on Hand
- Case studies (CMS project CPT) with real screenshots: illwerke vkw, Vorarlberg state hospitals (VLKH), FHE Gastro, Dr. Hamza dental practice, Martina Velmeden, Beschützerbox, der-raeumprofi.at.
- Testimonials (CMS testimonial CPT), toned not verbatim; originals in `docs/testimonials-2026-10-12.md`.
- Sourced claims table for service copy: `docs/services-copy-2026-11.md`.
- Absent and never to be invented: client counts beyond "more than 200 client sites maintained for a Swiss agency", conversion or speed multipliers, prices, awards.

## Product Principles
1. Proof over promises: show real sites and real client words, only measured numbers.
2. The person is the product: make clear the visitor deals with Edris directly.
3. Ownership and clarity: the client understands what they get and keeps control of it.
4. Equal quality in German and English; no regional targeting in headlines or titles.
5. Editable by Edris: no visible text hard-coded without a CMS field.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; touch targets at least 44px.
